How parts are listed

Every part on the shelf is logged with condition (New OEM, Used JP import, or Aftermarket), the casting or OEM number, confirmed fitment, and a warranty term before it goes live. Used JP imports are bench-tested and photographed at the counter; aftermarket parts are flagged clearly so buyers can choose. Prices are fixed at listing — the negotiation floor above sets how far a counter agent can go on a quote.
